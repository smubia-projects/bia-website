AI Lodge programme 

An 8-week program where participants, organized into teams known as lodges, explore and learn more about AI theory, applications and build with AI together. Participants are guided in creating their own personal AI projects. Designed to be accessible to individuals of all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced learners, AI Lodge provides a supportive environment for participants to experiment, learn, and grow while applying AI in meaningful and creative ways.

Weekly lodge sessions 
Hands-on learning workshops led by Lodge Captains, covering a different AI topic each week. Lodgers can explore foundational ML and AI concepts, build AI projects through guided labs, and experiment with automation tools and learn to vibecode. These sessions are designed to equip lodgers with practical AI skills, technical exposure, and project-building experience to support the development of their final project for the AI Lodge Hackathon.

AI lodge family 
The AI Lodge community is built on the belief that learning thrives in a close-knit, supportive environment. Beyond technical growth, we aim to foster a strong culture of shared learning, peer mentorship, and meaningful friendships, where lodgers grow alongside one another. AI Lodge is designed as a lasting ecosystem — one where members stay connected, give back, and pass on what they have learnt. Through lodgers becoming future Lodge Captains, we hope to cultivate a tradition of mentorship and continuity, strengthening the AI Lodge family across generations.

AI lodge Hackathon (NEW!)
Hack Day marks the official launch of the hackathon, where the problem statement is revealed and lodgers begin building their final projects in a collaborative session. After submissions, projects will be internally evaluated, with the top 10 selected as finalists for the Final Pitches.
Hackathon Finals Day showcases the projects built throughout the programme, highlighting lodgers’ innovation and AI skills. Finalists will pitch to a panel of judges, followed by a prize presentation ceremony and networking session.
